# Physical parameter status

- Manufacturer/paper scale: nominal operating mass, wheelbase, track, wheel radius, bucket width/capacity and steering range.
- Estimated: every pivot, shape, body mass allocation, COM, attachment limit, tire width, contact coefficient and equivalent rotary drive setting. YAML comments label their origin.
- Derived: diagonal uniform-box/cylinder inertia tensors from estimated body extents and allocated masses. Positive physical tensors do not establish correct inertias.
- Not implemented: hydraulic cylinders/ratios, engine/driveline/differentials, shared power, tire compliance and axle oscillation.
- Not calibrated: driving force, steering response, boom/curl speed under load, breakout force, stopping behavior and material interaction.
- Before M1 acceptance: obtain linkage drawings or measurements, body mass/COM/inertia data and cylinder/pressure/flow/stroke specifications; match measured trajectories, forces and slip under held-out conditions.
- Manufacturer bucket breakout force must not be copied as a cylinder force or rotary joint torque.
- Rigid-pile contact demonstrates obstacle interaction only. It cannot demonstrate penetration, material flow or bucket filling.
- Material YAMLs deliberately contain unknown values. Aoshima's dry gravel is not a calibration for dry sand, coal or moist sand.
- Newton loop/stop reference times and impedance are numerical stabilization settings; they do not calibrate hydraulic response or real mechanical-stop compliance.
- Newton rigid-ground diagnostic gates now pass using a smooth cylinder contact approximation and provisional 40 ms compliance reference. This is numerical stabilization, not measured tire deformation, stiffness or damping. No loaded driving, breakout or soil calibration is implied.
