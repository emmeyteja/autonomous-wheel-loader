# Physical parameter status

- Manufacturer/paper scale: nominal operating mass, wheelbase, track, wheel radius, bucket width/capacity and steering range.
- Estimated: every pivot, shape, body mass allocation, COM, attachment limit, tire width, contact coefficient and equivalent rotary drive setting. YAML comments label their origin.
- Derived: diagonal uniform-box/cylinder inertia tensors from estimated body extents and allocated masses. Positive physical tensors do not establish correct inertias.
- Not implemented: hydraulic cylinders/ratios, engine/driveline/differentials, shared power, tire compliance and axle oscillation.
- Not calibrated: driving force, steering response, boom/curl speed under load, breakout force, stopping behavior and material interaction.
- Before M1 acceptance: obtain linkage drawings or measurements, body mass/COM/inertia data and cylinder/pressure/flow/stroke specifications; match measured trajectories, forces and slip under held-out conditions.
- Manufacturer bucket breakout force must not be copied as a cylinder force or rotary joint torque.
- Rigid-pile contact demonstrates obstacle interaction only. It cannot demonstrate penetration, material flow or bucket filling.
- Material YAMLs deliberately contain unknown values. Aoshima's dry gravel is not a calibration for dry sand, coal or moist sand.
- Newton loop/stop reference times and impedance are numerical stabilization settings; they do not calibrate hydraulic response or real mechanical-stop compliance.
- Newton rigid-ground diagnostic gates now pass using a smooth cylinder contact approximation and provisional 40 ms compliance reference. This is numerical stabilization, not measured tire deformation, stiffness or damping. No loaded driving, breakout or soil calibration is implied.

## Data needed to replace the approximation

- Identify the exact WA320 variant, bucket option, tires and operating configuration. Overall machine mass alone cannot determine individual body dynamics.
- Highest priority: a licensed linkage/CAD drawing or measured pivot table, cylinder mounting coordinates, bucket/edge profile and joint travel/stroke stops. Include frame definitions and measurement uncertainty.
- Per moving assembly: mass, centre of mass and inertia tensor in a declared local frame. Manufacturer CAD mass properties, component measurements or identified equivalent values are preferable to uniform-box estimates.
- Actuators: bore/rod diameters, retracted pin length, stroke, pressure/flow/relief limits, speed/force under load, delays and any lift/tilt power sharing. Rotary drive torque cannot be equated with cylinder force without the verified linkage ratio.
- Tires/axles/driveline: effective loaded radius, width, inflation/deflection and slip data, axle oscillation geometry, speed/torque characteristics, differential behavior and power limits.
- Validation data: synchronized known controls, chassis/link/bucket motion, lift/tilt pressures or forces, payload and before/after pile scans. Separate fitting runs from held-out checks.
- Current cylinder clipping was caused by a behind-hinge visual mount, not evidence that the chassis needs a cutout. A visual cut does not change explicit inertia. A collision cut changes contacts; removing real structural material changes mass/COM/inertia and requires measured/CAD information. Preserve those distinctions and do not infer a manufacturer's chassis shape from our approximation.

- [Bucket registration/rod audit](../models/WA320_BUCKET_GEOMETRY.md) supports a side-profile candidate and preferred F interpretation, but the old D assignment fails rod-length consistency. Dump reach and ground-lip discrepancies remain explicit; no vehicle/cylinder forces are validated.
